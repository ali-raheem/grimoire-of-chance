        Dice notation

        2d6+3Two six-sided dice plus three
        d20adv / d20disAdvantage / disadvantage
        4d6dl1Drop the lowest one (also dh, kh, kl)
        4d6dl1 x6Repeat the whole roll six times
        (1d4+1) x3Three separate magic missiles
        2d6ro<3Reroll ones and twos once (r rerolls until it passes)
        d6!Exploding dice (!! compounds into one die)
        d100 / d%Percentile
        4dFFudge / Fate dice
        (2d6+4)/2Maths with parentheses; division rounds down
        d20min10Clamp each die (also max)
        adv, dis, statsShortcuts for d20adv, d20dis, 4d6dl1 x6

        Press / to jump to the expression box.

        Install for offline use

        On Android or desktop Chrome/Edge, use the Install button in the header or the browser menu. On iOS Safari, tap Share then Add to Home Screen. Everything works offline once loaded, and your sets, tables and history stay on this device.
